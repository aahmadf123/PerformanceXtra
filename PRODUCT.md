# Product

## Register

product

## Users
PerformanceXtra serves student athletes first, with coaches, admins, and super admins supporting that experience from different sides of the same system.

- Student athletes use the app in short, repeatable sessions to find assigned mental-performance work, complete reflections, check in on wellbeing, and stay connected to their coach without friction.
- Coaches use it during active roster management to assign focused work, monitor completion, spot athletes who need support, and keep communication and content management inside one place.
- Admins and super admins use it to keep the system operational, consistent, and safe across teams, accounts, content, and site-wide settings.

## Product Purpose
PerformanceXtra turns mental-performance training into a structured, repeatable workflow instead of a scattered mix of links, notes, and off-platform follow-up. It gives student athletes a clear next action, gives coaches a dependable operating surface for assignments and oversight, and gives admins a secure way to manage the program without developer intervention.

Success looks like this: athletes know what to do next, coaches can support many athletes without losing visibility, and the app feels steady, encouraging, and trustworthy across every role.

## Brand Personality
Encouraging, energetic, calm.

The product should feel supportive without becoming soft, motivating without becoming loud, and structured without becoming clinical. It should read as a serious performance tool for real athletes, while still making daily check-ins, assignment completion, and coach communication feel approachable.

## Anti-references
- Generic AI-looking SaaS dashboards that rely on interchangeable card grids, decorative gradients, and empty “modern” polish instead of clear workflow design.
- Over-gamified productivity apps that turn athlete support into pressure, streak obsession, or leaderboard energy.
- Sterile clinical wellness interfaces that feel diagnostic, cold, or medical.
- Aggressive sports branding that feels macho, chaotic, or visually noisy at the expense of clarity.

## Design Principles
- Keep the next action obvious: every role should immediately understand what matters now and what to do next.
- Support the coach-athlete relationship: communication, wellbeing, and assignments should feel connected, not like separate tools forced together.
- Encourage steady progress, not pressure: reinforce momentum with clarity and support, never with shame or overload.
- Earn trust through role-aware clarity: show only the controls each user needs, and make the system feel dependable and secure.
- Stay operational under real-world load: dense information is fine when it helps coaches and admins move faster, but it must remain readable and calm.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.2 AA across the product.

Design for keyboard-first operation, strong contrast, clear focus states, reduced-motion tolerance, and color choices that remain legible for color-blind users. Large text should scale cleanly, instructions should stay plainspoken, and supportive athlete-facing flows should avoid language that feels punitive or excluding.
